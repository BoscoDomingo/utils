# ASD-STE100 Writing Rules

This file summarises public ASD-STE100 rule categories from Issue 9, dated
January 2025. It does not reproduce the official standard or its approved
dictionary. Always consult the official standard for the most up-to-date information.

## Rule categories

### Word choice

- Use approved words only in their approved meaning and part of speech.
- Use one meaning for each word.
- Prefer the plainest, shortest, common word.

### Verb forms

- Use infinitive, imperative, simple present, simple past, simple future, and
  past participles used as adjectives.
- Do not use present perfect, past perfect, or other compound constructions.
- Use `-ing` forms only as technical nouns or as part of technical nouns.

### Voice

- Use active voice for procedures and instructions.
- Use passive voice only in descriptive text when the agent is unknown.

### Sentence structure

- Put one instruction in each sentence.
- Keep procedures and instructions to about 20 words.
- Keep descriptive sentences to about 25 words.
- Do not omit sentence parts to shorten a sentence.
- Keep noun clusters to three words or fewer.

### Paragraph and document structure

- Keep one topic in each paragraph.
- Keep paragraphs to six sentences or fewer.
- Use numbered or bulleted lists for sequences, conditions, and complex
  enumerations.

### Safety instructions

Start safety-critical instructions with a clear command or condition. Do not
bury the instruction in the middle of a sentence.
