# Why ASD-STE100

## What ASD-STE100 is

ASD-STE100 is a controlled natural language for technical documentation. The
standard was first released in 1986 as AECMA Document PSC-85-16598.

The Aerospace, Security and Defence Industries Association of Europe owns
ASD-STE100. The Simplified Technical English Maintenance Group maintains the
standard.

European airlines requested the standard because maintenance documentation had
to be clear for readers who might not be native English speakers.

## Structure

- 53 writing rules cover word choice, grammar, sentence structure, and style.
- The dictionary contains roughly 900 approved words.
- Each approved word has one meaning and one part of speech.
- The standard identifies roughly 1,200 words to avoid and gives replacements.
- Organisations can define approved technical terms beyond the base dictionary.

## Why this Skill uses ASD-STE100

ASD-STE100 helps readers who cannot ask the author a follow-up question. A user
may need to parse a tool description or inter-agent message without a human
author available to clarify it.

This Skill applies the principles to rewrite text or produce responses in ASD-STE100 style.
It does not transform live tool calls, hidden analysis, or internal agent
traffic.
